📌 Project Overview

India’s agricultural sector supports nearly half of the country’s workforce and contributes significantly to national GDP. However, smallholder farmers face growing risks due to climate variability, financial constraints, limited access to technology, and fragmented supply chains.

This project focuses on designing scalable, sustainable, and inclusive climate-resilience solutions to strengthen agricultural productivity and long-term farmer livelihoods across vulnerable regions in India

📈 Key Analyses & Findings
SWOT Highlights

Strengths: Large agricultural market, crop diversity, government initiatives

Weaknesses: Financial limitations, low scalability, fragmented supply chains

Opportunities: Affordable tools, blockchain transparency, community knowledge-sharing

Threats: Climate uncertainty, high upfront costs, resistance to technology adoption

Risk Analysis

High exposure to extreme weather events in regions such as Punjab and West Bengal

Overuse of fertilizers linked to declining soil health indices
